About Our Garden

BOTANICAL GARDEN

In the heart of COBACH 01 blooms the Botanical Garden, a space created not only for the study of nature, but also for a deep encounter with the living essence of the Baja California Sur desert.
This educational project was born from the desire to understand, preserve, and honor the native flora of Baja California Sur, protecting more than 150 plant species that silently tell the story of a land shaped by the sun, the wind, and resilience.
Every pathway and every plant invite visitors to contemplation and learning, reminding us that even in the driest landscapes, life flourishes with serene and extraordinary beauty.
